As a curious explorer of the brain, I seek to understand **how we become who we are** — from the molecular whispers that shape early development to the dynamic networks of the mature nervous system. My passion lies in developmental neuroscience, especially in uncovering how the circuits underlying perception, cognition, and decision-making are sculpted in the earliest stages of life — shaping the self, the collective, and the lifelong story of becoming.
